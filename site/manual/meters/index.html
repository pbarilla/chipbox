Not everything is in four. Give a page its own meter, and write a waltz.

1Open a page

Pick a channel in the RACK view and hit SEQ

2A few notes

Press a key to write a note and move on. Turn SELECT to skip a step
Put a note on steps 1, 5 and 9. That's the beat

3Make it a waltz

Turn KNOB 3 down to 3/4. The page is 12 steps now
Steps 13 to 16 get crossed out. They're skipped when the page plays
Hit PLAY. The dots under the tempo count three beats now
PROTIP: A note on a crossed-out step stays where it is, greyed out. Turn KNOB 3 back and it returns

4Other meters

Turn KNOB 3 to try 6/8 and 7/8. The stripes show where each group of beats starts
PROTIP: The meters go 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8 and 7/8. The knob stops at each end
PROTIP: A new page starts with the meter of the page before it
