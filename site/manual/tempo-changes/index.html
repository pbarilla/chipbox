Give a page its own tempo, and the song speeds up or slows down when it gets there.

1A second page

Hit HOME until you're in the SONG view
Hit OCT+ to add a slot with a new page. It keeps the meter of the page before it

2A tempo of its own

Tap EDIT to open the new page in the RACK view, then hit SEQ
Turn KNOB 1. The tempo turns amber. This page plays at its own tempo now

3Hear it

Hit HOME until you're in the SONG view, turn SELECT to slot 1 and hit PLAY. The tempo changes the moment page 2 starts
PROTIP: Turn KNOB 1 back to the song's tempo and the page lets go. The amber goes away
PROTIP: Outside step entry, KNOB 1 changes the tempo you see: the page's own if it has one, the whole song's if it doesn't
PROTIP: A new page starts with the tempo of the page before it
