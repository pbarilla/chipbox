ChipboxALPHA

Chiptune for the M-VAVE FM-1. NES, Game Boy and Master System sound chips, a step sequencer with arpeggios, and a screen that dresses up as each console.

Download Source

What it does

  • Three chips at once: NES, Game Boy, Master System. Nine channels.
  • The rack: 16 steps for every channel, 4 pages, an arpeggio on any step.
  • The chip screen: tone and envelope knobs for the channel you are on.
  • 8 projects saved to flash. The FM-1 opens the one you left.

Alpha: no noise channels, no tempo knob and no live record yet. Expect rough edges.

Install

  1. Easiest: the FM-1 Firmware Hub in Chrome or Edge, once Chipbox is listed there.
  2. Or download the .fwsc from Releases and, with Python 3:
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py chipbox-0.1.0-alpha.fwsc

Chipbox keeps its projects in the flash area other FM-1 firmwares use for theirs, so installing it and saving a project erases what they left there. To get back to stock use "Back to official" on the hub. If the FM-1 won't start, hold OCT- while switching it on and install again.

Controls

Press GLO on any screen for its help. HOME switches between the rack and the chip screen; hold it for your projects.
